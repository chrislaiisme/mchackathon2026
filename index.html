About the application

From an idea to a clearer apparel brief

Consumer Intent Agent is a non-commercial hackathon prototype operated by the 勢在必得 team. It helps people express apparel preferences through natural-language requests and image-based choices, then turns those interactions into structured signals for design exploration.

01

Understand

Extract explicit needs such as garment type, fit, color, material, and occasion.

02

Explore

Ask focused visual questions to clarify preferences that are difficult to express in words.

03

Visualize

Generate original, brand-neutral concept imagery based on the resulting preference brief.

Pinterest integration

Curated references, used responsibly

The application requests access only to Pinterest boards and Pins authorized by the connected account. Selected content may be used internally to identify apparel trend signals or as a visual reference for generating a new, original concept image.

  • Pinterest originals are not displayed publicly in the consumer experience.
  • We do not sell Pinterest data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not ask image-generation systems to reproduce logos, brands, or creators' work.
