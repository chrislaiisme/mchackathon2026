Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the 勢在必得 team handles information in the Consumer Intent Agent, a non-commercial prototype created for MCHackathon 2026.

1. Information we access

When an authorized user connects a Pinterest account, the application may access:

  • basic account information made available through Pinterest OAuth;
  • boards and board identifiers selected or available to the authorized account;
  • Pin metadata, including titles, descriptions, links, and media URLs; and
  • images from Pins that the authorized user selects for internal trend analysis or visual reference.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are credentials. They are stored separately from source code and are never published in this website or repository.

2. How we use information

We use authorized Pinterest data only to:

  • retrieve team-curated apparel references;
  • derive internal, aggregate apparel trend signals;
  • select relevant visual references when a user has not supplied one; and
  • create original, brand-neutral apparel concept images for prototype testing.

We do not sell Pinterest data, use it for targeted advertising, or publicly display downloaded Pinterest images in the consumer-facing experience.

3. Image generation and service providers

A selected reference image and a related text prompt may be sent to OpenAI through Hermes Agent using an authenticated OpenAI service. OpenAI processes that content to generate a new concept image. Pinterest provides the account authorization and Pin data used by the application. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Storage, retention, and security

Pinterest data and downloaded images are kept in access-controlled local project storage and are not committed to public source control. We retain them only while they are needed for prototype development, evaluation, or demonstration, and delete them when they are no longer required. We use reasonable technical measures to restrict access to tokens and cached content.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to operate the prototype, including Pinterest for API access and OpenAI for selected image-generation requests. We do not sell, rent, or disclose Pinterest data to advertisers or data brokers.

6. Your choices and deletion requests

You may revoke the application's access through Pinterest account settings. You may also ask us to delete locally stored Pinterest data and generated artifacts associated with your use by opening a request in our issue tracker. We will verify the request before removing account-linked data.

7. Children's privacy

This prototype is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Policy changes

We may update this policy as the prototype changes. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact the project maintainers through the project issue tracker.
